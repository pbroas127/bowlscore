**Description:**
Know what is really in the bowl.

BowlScore reads the label on any dog or cat food and gives it a clear score from 0 to 100, with the reasons in plain language.

HOW IT WORKS
Point your camera at the ingredients list or scan the barcode. In a few seconds you see the score, the ingredients worth watching, the good stuff, and how the nutrition stacks up.

SCORED FOR YOUR PET
Tell us about your pet once. BowlScore warns you when a food contains something your pet is allergic to, and checks protein and fat against what a puppy, kitten, adult or senior actually needs.

DOGS AND CATS ARE DIFFERENT
Cats must eat meat, need more protein than dogs, and need taurine. Some ingredients that are legal in dog food are prohibited in cat food. BowlScore uses separate rules for each.

WET AND DRY, COMPARED FAIRLY
A can is mostly water, so the numbers on the label look low. BowlScore removes the water first, the same way nutrition standards do, so canned food and kibble can be compared honestly.

A SCORE YOU CAN TRUST
Every point comes from the label. Brands cannot pay for a score, and the same label always gets the same score. Our method and sources are public on our website.

WHAT YOU GET

• Real, higher scoring foods suggested after every scan
• Side by side compare for any two foods
• A pantry for each pet, treats included
• A 7 day switch plan with daily reminders
• Recall alerts for the foods you feed
• Flags for artificial colors, synthetic preservatives, unnamed meats and more
• Nutrition shown on a dry matter basis
• Allergy alerts matched to each pet
• Every scan saved to your history

**App Review notes:**
BowlScore scores pet food from a photo of the ingredient label or a barcode. The app uses a hard paywall after onboarding; a sandbox purchase of either plan unlocks everything. Onboarding includes a demo scan with sample data so the core feature can be seen before the paywall. Account creation is optional (Settings, Account) and offers Sign in with Apple. Account and data deletion is in Settings, Legal, Delete my data. Scoring is rule based and the full method is public at https://bowlscoreapp.vercel.app/methodology. The app does not give medical advice and says so in the result screen and in Settings.

**Privacy nutrition label answers:**
Data linked to the user: email address (only if they create an account), user ID, purchase history (RevenueCat), user content (pet profiles and scan history, only when signed in). Photos: sent to our server for label reading, not stored, not linked. No tracking, no third party advertising.
