# BowlScore marketing plan (from the Cal AI playbook)

Sources: 10 YouTube transcripts pulled 2026-09-22 (Cal AI head of product on Subversive, Zach Yadegari interview, Cal AI ad teardown of 131 ads, onboarding/paywall teardown, Sway 0 to $2M, Glamour/Sprout $300K MRR, Profit AI "Cal AI for finance", BrainRot copy video).

## What Cal AI actually did
1. Picked the channel before the product: "can this be shown in 15 seconds?" Snap food, see calories. Marketing started in month 2 with a bare bones app.
2. Creators, not ads, first. Hundreds of fitness creators (300 posting in one month). Creators embody the result and promoting a tracker does not compete with their own business.
3. Picked creators by their WORST recent video, consistency and real comments, not one viral hit.
4. Pay: minimum view requirement, bonus above targets; $100 for a 50K view video ($2 CPM) vs about $5 revenue per 1K views. Bundles (4 videos for $400) got cheaper each post.
5. Only two rules for creators: show scan, analyzing, result screens, and do it early in the video. Everything else is the creator's style.
6. Product built for the camera: big legible text, logo on every core screen, a separate creator build with a perfect streak and meal history.
7. Reposted creator videos on their own accounts. Top 10% of organic videos became ads (Spark ads, $30 to $50 a day, broad targeting). Ads made specifically to sell (hook in 5 s, demo, "here's the app I use") scaled to $40K/day; subtle organic clips capped at $10 to 15K/day.
8. Affiliates paid a share of revenue made ad specific videos. Meta pixel optimized for trial start. Custom App Store product page per ad for attribution.
9. Onboarding: first screen is the same scan video people saw on TikTok, same video on the paywall, social proof between questions, "what's stopping you", referral code step, cheap price ($30/yr, 3 day trial) so people recommend it freely.
10. Word of mouth: streaks, milestones, share cards, $10 referral. Seasonal moments (Thanksgiving free access).

## BowlScore version

### Product tweaks (one build, cheap)
- Creator mode: a demo toggle with big logo, big score, legible text, a full history (the screenshot demo data already exists).
- Onboarding screen 1 and the paywall show a 5 second clip: scan a bag, score slams in.
- Add "App Store" and "Friend" to "where did you hear about us", plus an optional referral or creator code (attribution).
- Share card for "I scanned my dog's food" with the score and the logo, one tap to Stories.

### Phase 0: accounts (after Apple approves)
- Create TikTok, Instagram, X, Pinterest, Threads (option 1 already agreed). Warm up 3 to 7 days: follow and watch only pet food, dog, cat content.
- Build a swipe file: small accounts (200 to 1K avg views) with one pet video over 500K. Copy the format, not the content.

### Phase 1: faceless slideshows, $0, weeks 1 to 4
- 1 to 2 posts a day. Formats: "I scanned the 10 most popular dog foods", "the food my vet recommended scored...", "3 ingredients to check on your dog's bag", "cheap vs expensive kibble", "cat food with propylene glycol is banned for a reason", before and after swap.
- Screenshots come from the existing puppeteer pipeline. Captions stick to label facts and our public rubric.
- Judge by installs per 1K views (target 10 per 1K), not views.

### Phase 2: micro creators, about $100 to $300 a month
- Pet accounts (dog moms, breed accounts, groomers, trainers) with 10K+ views on their worst recent posts. Avoid creators sponsored by a pet food brand.
- Offer $10 to $30 base with a 500 view minimum, plus about $1 to 2 per 1K views bonus, or 4 video bundles. DM 50 a day.
- Brief: show scan, loading, score in the first 5 s; rest is theirs. Give each a creator code.
- Repost every creator video on our accounts.

### Phase 3: paid, only on proven winners
- When an organic video beats 10 installs per 1K views, Spark ad it at $30 to $50 a day, broad targeting, optimize for trial start.
- Then make ad specific cuts: hook, demo, "this is the app I use for my dog".

### Moments
- Halloween (chocolate and treats), Thanksgiving leftovers "can my dog eat this", Christmas treats, New Year pet goals, pet food recall news (we already have the recall feed).

### Guardrails
- Scores only from the label and the public rubric; show the label in the video. No "this brand is poison" claims.
- Disclose Amazon affiliate links and paid creator posts (#ad).
- Not vet advice. No fake reviews or fake testimonials.
- Never spend Gemini or OpenAI on content; screenshots and slides are free.
